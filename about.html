Hi, I'm Aaron

My role model is Santa. He sets expectations, empathizes with his audience, and delivers.

That’s most of my philosophy. I’m a UX designer, and the work I’m proudest of has been for users the industry designs past — people who open an app and assume that if it’s confusing, the fault is theirs. It isn’t. It’s mine to fix.

I have three little girls I’d do pretty much anything for. I’ve loved fantasy and sci-fi since I was a kid, and I still write my own stories.
